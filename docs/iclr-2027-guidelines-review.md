## Suggested wording

These passages are proposals for organizer review, not assertions that the policies have already been adopted.

### In-person format and access

> We will organize a single-day, in-person workshop. For exceptional circumstances such as visa or travel constraints, we will accommodate contributors through prerecorded presentations, posters displayed on their behalf, and remote presentations where venue facilities permit. Accepted papers, posters, and short presentation videos will be made available on the workshop website, with author permission, to support access beyond the event.

### Mandatory publication and import deadline

> By 26 February 2027, 11:59 pm AoE, we will notify all contributors of their acceptance status, make all accepted papers publicly available on OpenReview, and import them into iclr.cc. This deadline will not be extended.

### Conflicts of interest

> Organizers will recuse themselves from the review, discussion, and acceptance decisions for conflicted submissions, including submissions from anyone within the same organization. An unconflicted organizer or an independent program committee member will oversee the review and decision. Organizers will not give talks at the workshop, except brief opening or closing remarks, and may moderate the panel.

### Talk titles and schedules

> We will publish talk titles and the workshop's archival policy before ICLR's workshop site publication. We will update opening and closing times on iclr.cc by 26 March 2027 and finalize the schedule in the organizer portal by 14 April 2027, both at 11:59 pm AoE.

### Short submissions, if adopted

> We welcome submissions of up to four pages, single column, excluding references, including shorter papers presenting early-stage ideas, modest self-contained results, follow-up experiments, reanalyses, and new perspectives on existing work. Short submissions will be primarily human-authored; AI assistance may be disclosed, but AI-generated papers are not permitted for the tiny/short-paper initiative.

### Proposal AI disclosure

> Generative AI tools assisted with assembling and editing organizer-supplied proposal text, formatting the Markdown and LaTeX files, comparing the draft with official workshop guidelines, and implementing the workshop website. The organizers are responsible for reviewing and approving the final text, factual claims, policies, and artifacts before submission.

## Submission format and dates

The official guide and call, and the live submission form checked here, do not specify a mandatory LaTeX template or a maximum proposal page count. The generated standard-article LaTeX file is therefore not contradicted by these sources. It has not been compiled locally, so its actual pagination is unverified. The four-page limit in the proposal applies to workshop contributions, not the proposal itself.

The live form requires a PDF upload of at most 50 MB, as well as a title, organizers, keywords, an abstract of at most 5,000 characters, speaker/panelist IDs, reviewer recommendation IDs, a workshop-requirements confirmation, and a CC BY 4.0 license. The TL;DR field is optional and limited to 250 characters. Upload the compiled PDF, not the Markdown file or Overleaf ZIP.
